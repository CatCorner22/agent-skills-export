## Key claims

- Zuckerberg on larger, more-expensive Wonder; free Muse primarily Spark. Future equal-capability smaller model is aspirational. `"we’re going to start off primarily in paid subscriptions."` (Zuckerberg/Wang interview, 2026-10-12; cheekypint.txt L528).
- Wang’s Active Memory qualification; model plus system, not perfect persistent recall. `"there’s still plenty of room to improve."` (Zuckerberg/Wang interview, 2026-10-12; cheekypint.txt L456).
- Wang identifies internal measurement; not external corroboration. `"we have our own internal evals of Memory Bench"` (Zuckerberg/Wang interview, 2026-10-12; cheekypint.txt L396).
- Zuckerberg describes privacy/discretion goal when reserving restaurants; not proof of enforcement. `"if you have an allergy or you’re pregnant or something"` (Zuckerberg/Wang interview, 2026-10-12; cheekypint.txt L652).
- Zuckerberg: browser fallback intended to work without partner integration; partners can provide direct APIs. `"we trained into the models very good browser use"` (Zuckerberg/Wang interview, 2026-10-12; cheekypint.txt L1480).
- Wang vendor benchmark claim; interview is commercially interested, not independent measurement. `"state-of-the-art on DeepSWE and, SWE-Marathon"` (Zuckerberg/Wang interview, 2026-10-12; cheekypint.txt L496).
- Zuckerberg’s MMA video-coaching latency constraint, not a general API latency statistic. `"you only get 1 minute in between the rounds."` (Zuckerberg/Wang interview, 2026-10-12; cheekypint.txt L88).
