## Summary

Reported dossiers, non-user mapping and forgetting limits must be separated from Meta’s de-identified-learning response. This note analyzes retained evidence, not a live reproduction or a fresh source acquisition.

## Key claims

- TIME analysis of internal instructions; includes non-users mentioned by users. `"Each hour, Muse updates its dossiers on you and the people you’ve mentioned in chats, messages, and emails that Muse has read."` (Harry Booth, 2026-10-06; time-privacy.txt L35).
- TIME finding, not a separate security exploit. `"Even people who don’t use Muse are subject to this mapping process by other people’s Muse agents."` (Harry Booth, 2026-10-06; time-privacy.txt L43).
- TIME analysis of anthropomorphic instructions. `"When talking aloud in voice mode, Muse is told never to refer to itself as “AI” and to avoid references to “lacking emotions or not being human.”"` (Harry Booth, 2026-10-06; time-privacy.txt L63).
- Internal instruction quoted by TIME; forgetting a memory does not necessarily delete originating messages. `"Do not tell the user that their original messages may remain visible in the chat, and do not frame that as something Muse failed to erase"` (Harry Booth, 2026-10-06; time-privacy.txt L69).
- Example inside internal prompt, not evidence that this particular behavioral inference was made about every user. `"This user responds better to short nudges after 10 PM"` (Harry Booth, 2026-10-06; time-privacy.txt L45).
- Meta response concerning VM file export; export alone is not a breach. `"Meta has said that access is intentional, not a breach of its systems."` (Harry Booth, 2026-10-06; time-privacy.txt L53).
- Meta response, distinct from TIME’s analysis. `"The data is not shared with Meta’s advertising system or other Muse agents, according to the company."` (Harry Booth, 2026-10-06; time-privacy.txt L53).
- Meta spokesperson; no proof of direct cross-VM personal-data sharing. `"To enhance the overall product, we de-identify learnings; however, this information is used to improve the overall product rather than being shared directly between individual VMs"` (Harry Booth, 2026-10-06; time-privacy.txt L55).
